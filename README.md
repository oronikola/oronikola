# AdaptiveHub

We are the engineering team behind the **Adaptive** family of products: multi-tenant software for schools, libraries, research institutions, transport cooperatives and payments. Every product is built to scale across many organisations from one platform, with real-time web and mobile experiences and AI where it actually helps.

---

## 🏫 School platforms

* **Kora**: One platform for every school, from pre-school to college. A school management system for Philippine Basic Ed, Senior High, College and TVET: admissions and enrollment, registrar and academic records, grading, attendance, finance, cashiering, accounting and official forms, each with its own portal. Multi-tenant on PostgreSQL with row-level security.
* **[AdaptiveHub](http://adaptivehub.app/landing)**: The adaptive, gamified, AI-assisted LMS for K-12 and higher education. Assessments with rubric and AI essay grading, topic-mastery tracking and remedial generation, XP, badges and leaderboards, report cards, and real-time updates.
  * **AdaptiveHub Mobile**: the Flutter app for students and teachers on Android and iOS.
  * **AdaptiveHub Meet**: protected classroom video conferencing on LiveKit.
  * **AdaptiveHub MCP**: the AI backend: LLM routing, queues and curriculum-grounded mastery question generation.
  * **Ebook Toolkit**: prepares AdaptiveHub ebooks and generates and reviews mastery questions from their text.
* **[AdaptiveHub Events](https://events.adaptivehub.app)**: Live competitions: quiz rounds, Tetris and Type Racer rounds and the Quiz Runner game. Anyone can join from a shared pixel-art lobby, no account needed.
* **[AdaptiveLibrary](https://library.adaptivehub.app)**: Digital and physical library management for schools. Catalog, circulation, reservations and fines, access-controlled ebook lending, publisher accounts, orders and payments, and AI-assisted cataloguing, with a mobile app (Expo) for library members.
* **Adaptive Station**: School gate attendance. Students tap their ID at the gate and families see arrivals and departures as they happen in the mobile app, which also works as the school's SMS gateway.
* **Adaptive Human Resource**: Scheduling and recruitment automation, multi-tenant from day one.
* **Adaptive Help Center**: Real-time customer support, ticketing, and WebRTC audio and video calls for the whole Adaptive ecosystem.

## 🔬 Research and innovation

* **EDUCENTRA**: The academic and innovation platform: institutions, research, intellectual property, extension programs, problem matching, partnerships, investors, startups and the i-hub incubation pipeline.

## 🚐 Transport

* **KAWAY**: A platform for transport cooperatives. Drivers join their coop, register their vehicle and route and go live on the map; commuters see routes, fares and the next vehicle, mark where they are waiting and request rides. Includes a portal for coop administrators and a Flutter app with driver and commuter versions.

## 💳 Payments

* **AdaptivePay**: Billing and payments, with a client portal for statements of account and invoices and a console for staff.
